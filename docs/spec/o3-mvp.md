# O3 implementation contract

## Architecture and privacy

The Chrome extension provides a read-only room; a separately configured Home Assistant integration monitors NWS independently. Public JSON examples contain New York City and Washington, DC only. Browser settings and exports include watched locations and are sensitive. Private JSON working files belong under `ignored-resources/private-config/` and must not enter release bundles or Git history.

## Room

Collect NWS active alerts, USGS `all_hour` earthquake reports, and BBC feeds using packaged adapters. NWS and USGS target one-minute polling and BBC five-minute polling, subject to provider, network, and browser scheduling delays. USGS reports are post-event, not earthquake early warning. Display source health, times, stale and failed states, and coarse location associations without implying a household impact. Preserve bounded collections, safe links, and validation. Room settings reside in Chrome storage; a changed JSON file requires import to update that storage.

The optional `room.context_dashboard` is a non-authoritative third-party display link, initially World Monitor. It is independent of the source collectors and never authorizes an alarm. Legacy `primary_dashboard` imports remain supported. Fresh current NWS items receive retention priority within bounded history; non-expired retained items from a disabled, failed, or overdue source carry a visible cached badge (`Cached · source unavailable`), including NWS items also marked `Not in latest results` (missing or unknown NWS lifecycle defaults to cached when unavailable, whereas `expired` renders only `Source expiry passed`). All items have a seven-day collection-age bound. Global-cap history loss is disclosed separately from freshness and provider truncation, with a seven-day last-loss window and a reset when all previous source identities are removed. Disabled sources display neutral health and are excluded from enabled-source health rollups; an all-disabled collector is neutral. Startup collects overdue sources, and manual collection updates cadence. Bundled configuration failures produce an explicit room error.

## Home Assistant

**Qualification and per-feature quarantine.** Use current actual NWS evidence issued within the seven-day maximum acceptance age (`MAX_ACCEPTANCE_AGE`) with internally consistent full CAP identity/reference tuples, strictly earlier referenced issue times, a `sent` timestamp within five minutes of current clock time, a configured household point within supported geometry, an approved event and protective-instruction rule, and a valid action window for qualification. Invalid current Alert/Update identities or reference metadata (including future-dated `sent` >5 minutes ahead), as well as malformed, oversized, or conflicting-duplicate non-cancellation features, are quarantined per feature/lineage without failing unrelated valid alerts, preserve pending retries in suspension, and cannot establish raw grouping edges that suppress independently valid alerts or sibling branches; invalid `Actual Cancel` records (including future-dated `Cancel` messages) isolate recoverable reference targets in `uncertain_targets` (matching exact replays via persisted identity/target keys without writing unverified references into persistent `lineage` edges and without flipping to authoritative cancellation once the clock advances). When a malformed or oversized non-Cancel feature still has a valid feature `id` (`https://api.weather.gov/alerts/...`), even if `properties` is non-object/null or a reference `@id` is invalid/oversized, quarantine is scoped to `[feature_id, *valid_refs]`; `quarantine_ambiguous` is triggered only when a feature's own `id` cannot be safely extracted or when the poll's 5,000-unique-identifier quarantine cap is exceeded. Unknown geometry, missing footprints, tests, cancellations and expired alerts cannot qualify. Reconcile updates and cancellation, deduplicate equivalent active lineage, persist pending targets in a private HA `Store(..., private=True)`, and recheck eligibility before delivery. First plans target all configured phones and only affected-location speakers. Both live and commissioning gates must be explicitly true before output calls; default to shadow. Equivalent inactive actions bridge at most 15 minutes; changed event/instruction invalidates earlier pending work.

**CAP cancellation and continuing segments.** CAP references are directed message evidence; shared ancestry may group equivalent actions but never authorizes whole-component cancellation. Explicitly identified cancelled messages (including retained `state == "cancelled"` records whose original `Cancel` was evicted) remain blocked against stale replay within the seven-day horizon and transition to `ended_or_absent` once absent past that horizon. An unreferenced earlier or undated successor whose cancellation scope is uncertain is quarantined, has its pending outputs suspended, and contributes partial health (`cancellation_uncertain`) while current or active; once absent and outside a partial/quarantined poll, historic uncertainty resolves to `ended_or_absent` (while any remaining suspended outbox retry remains suspended until equivalent active recovery, invalidation, or the 15-minute outbox limit) rather than stalling unrelated monitoring. After a partial cancellation, a valid unreferenced continuing segment issued at or after the cancellation timestamp (with complete CAP tuples and no missing intermediate ancestor or prior uncertain target) has any repeating pre-cancellation action withheld/suspended in `quarantined` state, while independently qualifying new actions (`fp` not previously planned pre-cancellation, or where no prior action was planned) evaluate normally as `active` and plan without being blocked by the earlier root's `cancelled` tombstone. Independently supported sibling branches remain eligible for their own checks and retries. Do not infer cancellation scope from area names, matching VTEC, or overlapping UGC. A later Update explicitly referencing a cancellation must independently pass qualification before it can begin a new action.

**Retention bounds and scoped cancellation quarantine.** Persist directed identities, references and cancellation evidence across restarts for the seven-day acceptance and cancellation-evidence horizon from the latest component timestamp, and treat missing or conflicting lineage evidence as uncertainty rather than an all-clear. The journal is bounded to 1,000 records, 100 references per alert, and 100 current action fingerprints per record; overflow prioritizes active/current/`Cancel`/`cancelled` records ahead of ordinary history and extends `degraded_until` to cover the latest `dropped_horizon` across evicted components without shrinking an earlier longer horizon. When a malformed, oversized, or conflicting `Actual` `Cancel` has recoverable non-empty target reference URLs within the poll's 5,000-identifier quarantine cap (counting unique feature and reference IDs once per poll), quarantine only those targeted lineages and their directed descendants (`cancellation_uncertain`, `partial`) without blocking independent alerts.

**Outbox overflow and degraded-mode latching.** If bounded journal storage overflows (`cancellation_evidence_overflow`), an `Actual` `Cancel` lacks recoverable target reference URLs or exceeds the 5,000-identifier quarantine cap (`malformed_cancellation`), conflicting cancellation evidence lacks recoverable target scope or exceeds the 5,000-identifier quarantine cap (`conflicting_cancellation`), a previously initialized config entry's persisted journal goes missing (`journal_lost`), or the persisted journal fails JSON decoding, version/minor-version validation (`UnsupportedStorageVersionError` / `NotImplementedError` / rejected migration), or deep schema validation including `bool` checks on `initialized`/`was_live` and validation of `degraded_reason` against allowed persisted degraded reasons (`malformed_store`, marking `journal_initialized` on any non-empty or malformed store load; transient `OSError` raises `ConfigEntryNotReady` for retry), retain a durable evidence/storage degraded state (`evidence_degraded: true`, `output_blocked: true`) with explicit `degraded_reason`, `degraded_at`, and `degraded_until` (at least `MAX_ACCEPTANCE_AGE` / latest `dropped_horizon`, or `null` with stable `degraded_at` for `malformed_store`) and withhold all household output while continuing read-only collection (except for `malformed_store`, which skips collection to preserve corrupt disk files). Separately, when queued outbox targets would exceed the 5,000-target bound (`outbox_overflow`), the integration drains one bounded 100-target batch first when deliverable items exist in the outbox, queues new whole plans atomically up to 5,000 targets while stripping and withholding overflowing plans (`withheld_count`), continues bounded delivery (up to 100 targets per 10-target-group saved batch, 30-second deadline) of already-queued targets on poll 1 and subsequent polls without freezing item age under `last_degraded_poll`, sets `degraded_reason: "outbox_overflow"` with `degraded_until = now + MAX_AGE` (15 minutes), `evidence_degraded: false`, `output_blocked: false`, `partial: true`, active `active_count`, and accurate `deliverable_count`/`suspended_count`, and automatically clears `outbox_overflow` once all current qualifying plans fit within 5,000 targets. Across all degraded modes, raise an operator-visible Home Assistant persistent notification (`situareness_degraded`) once per degraded reason transition immediately on load and pre-fetch without re-creating on advancing timestamps (naming `degraded_until` and recovery via `acknowledge_degraded_at` + reload for non-`malformed_store` modes, or manual disk store repair/replacement for `malformed_store`; persisting `journal_lost` with stable `journal_lost_at` before fetching and keeping `journal_lost_at` on the entry through acknowledgement until the first post-recovery save completes; and dismissing `situareness_degraded` on recovery, unload, or before deleting the store on entry removal), accurately surface `cancellation_uncertain` across both degraded and healthy sensor snapshots (whenever a cancellation trigger occurred on that poll, scoped cancel quarantines or recoverable prior cancel conflicts are present, or quarantined records hold `"cancellation scope uncertain"`), report `withheld_count` (including plans stripped by `CancellationEvidenceOverflow`) alongside `planned_count` on degraded polls, and exclude `degraded_at`/`degraded_until` from recorder churn. Active outbox delivery persists once per completed 10-target delivery group (within the 100-target/poll, 15-minute `MAX_AGE` throughput limit of ~1,500 targets per 15 minutes).

**Recovery, notifications, and lifecycle.** Uncorrupted evidence/journal degraded states clear after `degraded_until` expires on a clean poll or via a one-shot non-future `acknowledge_degraded_at` evaluated solely at coordinator load/reload against load time (`degraded_at <= acknowledge_degraded_at <= load_now`), once active feed triggers (`malformed_cancellation`, `conflicting_cancellation`) or protected-record overflow (`cancellation_evidence_overflow`) are no longer present (since those ongoing triggers refresh `degraded_at` and `degraded_until` on each poll without shortening `degraded_until`). `config.validate()` rejects future `acknowledge_degraded_at` timestamps (`ack_ts > now`), and coordinator load consumes any non-matching `acknowledge_degraded_at` value (`acknowledged_degraded_at = ack`) so a stale or premature timestamp left in configuration can never clear a subsequent unseen degradation after reload or clock catch-up; `outbox_overflow` clears automatically once space frees up (or when `MAX_AGE` expires or via valid load-time acknowledgement), and `malformed_store` persists until repaired in `.storage/situareness.<entry_id>`, replaced by deleting the store file and any `.corrupt.*` backups (or replacing its data with empty JSON `{}`) on disk (which transitions to `journal_lost`), or cleared by removing and re-adding the integration entry. Resetting or replacing a corrupt or lost store (`malformed_store` / `journal_lost`), removing/re-adding the entry, or acknowledging `cancellation_evidence_overflow` early, discards or truncates historical tombstones and may allow previously seen or cancelled alerts still in their acceptance window to re-evaluate or re-alert. Alerts whose plans were withheld while degraded or overflowing (with fingerprints cleared from `records`), as well as undelivered pre-degradation outbox targets whose retry budget was blocked by evidence/storage degradation (preserving `last_degraded_poll` until post-recovery outbox migration), are evaluated for delivery once resolved without duplicating pre-degradation deliveries. See [CAP source evidence and policy](../reference/cap-cancellation.md). Ambiguous quarantine (unextractable feature `id` or >5,000 unique quarantined identifiers per poll) suspends all lineages for that poll and raises `situareness_degraded` while remaining active. Unload cancels in-flight fetches on stop (returning non-degraded stopped health with queued targets accounted as suspended when healthy) and stops later saves; entry removal dismisses `situareness_degraded` before deleting the private journal and any `.corrupt.*` backups. No horn adapter is implemented. Do not equate service acceptance with human receipt.

## Research and verification

`config/source-catalog.example.json` is a research catalog and is not consumed at runtime. The source skill can maintain a local catalog and propose supported room changes with validation; additional sources need adapters. Run offline checks, isolated browser tests and isolated actual Home Assistant tests as appropriate. Record exact results, dependencies, warnings, and untested physical behavior. Live commissioning is separate.
