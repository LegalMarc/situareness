# Situareness for Home Assistant

This custom integration is uncommissioned. It independently polls NWS alerts once a minute and defaults to shadow mode. Actual installation and delivery require separate commissioning. Continue to use independent official alert channels. Provider and network delays, polling, controller outages and device behavior can delay or prevent an alert.

For a future authorized installation, copy `custom_components/situareness` from this archive to Home Assistant's `<config>/custom_components/situareness`, restart HA, then add **Situareness** under Settings > Devices & services. The optional `home-assistant/situareness.example.json` is a blank starting point for the private `<config>/situareness.json` file. Keep a local working copy under `ignored-resources/` and copy it privately to HA; reload the integration after changes. The example queries NY and DC and contains no configured footprints, rules, or recipients. Never share or commit private coordinates or device identifiers.

Configure private household longitude/latitude points under `locations`, never town centers. Optional location `name` supplies the display label and `speakers` contains HA media player entity IDs. Configure `approved_rules` as exact NWS event names mapped to reviewed starting phrases of official protective instructions. Unconfigured points, absent polygons, unmatched wording, and unapproved rules cannot produce an alarm. `phones` lists HA mobile-app service names without `notify.`; `tts_entity` identifies an HA TTS entity. Rules and targets require validation before relying on them.

Both `live_enabled` and `commissioning_passed` must be explicitly true before output services can be called. Both are false in the supplied example. Leave them false until policy replay, private footprint and source verification, coordinated phone and speaker testing, and failure/stop behavior are documented and separately authorized. These settings do not themselves prove commissioning. There is no horn, Ring, police/fire panic, recipient alert acknowledgement, or repeated-reminder adapter.

New qualifying alerts target all configured phones and speakers at affected locations only. Phone service data includes the official URL and is limited to 2,800 JSON bytes; long instructions are shortened with a prompt to open the source, and large location lists can be summarized. The full instruction remains in the private outbox. Each speaker names its own location, omits the URL, and limits spoken instructions to 300 characters with a prompt to open the source link if shortened. The budget leaves provider-envelope headroom but does not establish final APNs size or delivery.

The integration queries NWS active GeoJSON alerts across configured states under bounded pagination (`50` pages) and response sizes (`8 MiB`/page), deduplicating identical feature copies without failing large multi-state bursts. It prioritizes new first-attempt targets ahead of retries and makes up to three total delivery attempts per target (`MAX_ATTEMPTS = 3`: one initial call plus up to two retries) for unsuccessful or uncertain calls within fifteen minutes (`MAX_AGE`)—retries of failed calls rather than repeated reminders—after checking the alert is still current (`100` target attempts/poll, `30`-second deadline, coalesced to at most one store save per 10-target group; when a near-capacity poll above `5,000` projected targets triggers a pre-drain before queueing, newly queued first-attempt items deliver in any remaining per-poll budget `100 - pre_drain_attempts` within the 30-second deadline and wait until the next poll if pre-drain exhausts all `100` poll attempts or the 30-second deadline; because outbox items carry up to 10KB instructions, a 5,000-target outbox can serialize tens of MB across up to ~12 saves/poll, and unexhausted queued targets for a currently active or suspended-from-active alert that age past 15 minutes—e.g., above ~1,500 attempts/15m or under slow service timeouts—or fail validation on backward clock rollback (`created > now`) or target removal are surfaced via persisted `expired_unsent`, `partial: true`, `outcomes` (`expired: true`), and `situareness_degraded` latched for seven days from the latest expiration or until cleared on reload via `acknowledge_degraded_at`). During non-`outbox_overflow` degraded modes (`7`-day+ holds), `created` ages freeze via `last_degraded_poll`, so pre-degradation retries can deliver days later once degradation clears if the alert remains active and qualifying. Expiration-only updates do not trigger a new alarm, and `ended_or_absent` transitions do not establish an official cancellation. Before each bounded delivery group, the action window is rechecked against the current clock; official cancellation is learned only on a later source poll, so a cancellation issued between polls is not immediately known. A crash after HA accepts a service call but before persistence can duplicate that group. Acceptance is not proof a person heard it.

## Health, storage, and degraded recovery

- **Sensor state and attributes:** `sensor.situareness_monitoring` exposes `active_count`, `event_count`, `source_feature_count`, `last_success`, `shadow`, `planned_count`, `withheld_count`, `pending_count`, `deliverable_count`, `suspended_count`, `delivery_attempts`, `delivery_failures`, `expired_unsent`, `partial`, `quarantined_count`, `quarantine_ambiguous`, `cancellation_uncertain`, `evidence_degraded`, `output_blocked`, `degraded_reason`, `degraded_at`, `degraded_until`, `acknowledge_rejected`, and up to 25 redacted `events` (excluding `events`, `last_success`, `degraded_at`, and `degraded_until` from recorder history).
- **Private storage and bounds:** State uses Home Assistant's `Store(..., private=True)` (`0600` when supported); post-fetch candidate resolution, `reconcile`, outbox migration/planning, deliverable-count checks, and store-schema validation (requiring every persisted record `state` and any non-`null` `prior_state` to belong to known states: `active`, `ineligible`, `ended_or_absent`, `quarantined`, `unconfirmed`, `superseded`, `cancelled`) run via `async_add_executor_job`. Removing the entry dismisses notifications before deleting the journal and any `.corrupt.*` backups while keeping `situareness.json`; the first successful save after recovering from `malformed_store` also automatically removes stale `.corrupt.*` backup files (or logs a warning and retries cleanup on subsequent saves if unlinking fails, without misclassifying a later missing store as `malformed_store`). Caps are `1,000` records (retention bounded at `min(ts, updated + 5m) + 7d` `MAX_ACCEPTANCE_AGE` and clamped at `MAX_TIMESTAMP`, with `protected` retaining cancellation components, all `active`/`quarantined`/`unconfirmed`/`cancelled` lineages, and `<=15m` bridge-window `actions` records even when absent, while excluding ordinary `ineligible` items and non-actionable hidden `superseded` placeholders outside actionable/cancellation groups, with safe snapshot fallback for evicted `ineligible` items), `100` references/alert, `100` fingerprints/record, `5,000` unique quarantined IDs/poll, and `5,000` outbox targets. Scoped quarantine isolates malformed/oversized features with valid IDs (quarantining `feature_id` and absent unreferenced prior records in `valid_refs` for non-`Cancel` features, while only `Actual` `Update`/`Cancel` messages can supersede referenced alerts); continuing segments at or after a partial cancellation (`sent >= cancelled_at`) can qualify new actions while pre-cancellation retries stay suspended.
- **Evidence and storage degraded modes (`evidence_degraded: true`, `output_blocked: true`, `active_count: unknown`):** Triggered by `cancellation_evidence_overflow` (`>1,000` protected records; `degraded_until` at least `7` days and covers evicted protected/cancellation component horizons up to `MAX_TIMESTAMP`), `malformed_cancellation` / `conflicting_cancellation` (an `Actual Cancel` lacks recoverable target references or exceeds the `5,000`-ID quarantine cap, or conflicting cancellation evidence lacks recoverable scope; retains `degraded_reason` precedence over simultaneous overflow with `degraded_until >= max(7d, overflow_horizon)`), `journal_lost` (missing store on an initialized entry; persisted pre-fetch with `7` days from non-future detection clamped to `MAX_TIMESTAMP`), or `malformed_store` (corrupt JSON, `.corrupt.*` backups when not already recovered, unsupported major version, outbox `>5,000`, missing/unknown record `state`, or schema error; skips collection and writes with `degraded_until: null`; transient `OSError` retries via `ConfigEntryNotReady`). Poll-wide `quarantine_ambiguous` (when a feature ID cannot be extracted or unique quarantined IDs exceed `5,000` in a poll) suspends all lineages for that poll and raises `situareness_degraded`.
- **Capacity mode (`outbox_overflow`, `evidence_degraded: false`, `output_blocked: false`, `partial: true`):** When queued plus new targets exceed `5,000`, drains `100` deliverable targets first if available, queues whole plans atomically up to `5,000`, strips/withholds overflowing plans (`withheld_count`), continues bounded `100`-target/poll queued delivery (first-attempt items ahead of retries) under a `15`-minute `degraded_until` without freezing item age, and auto-clears once capacity fits.
- **Operator recovery (`acknowledge_degraded_at` and `.storage`):**
  - While the same non-`malformed_store` reason persists, `degraded_at` stays at initial latch time while `degraded_until` extends.
  - Non-`malformed_store` modes clear when `degraded_until` expires on a clean poll, or on reload after setting a non-future timezone-aware ISO-8601 `acknowledge_degraded_at` in `situareness.json` with `degraded_at <= acknowledge_degraded_at <= reload time` (latched healthy `expired_unsent` clears after seven days or on reload with `expired_unsent_at <= acknowledge_degraded_at <= reload time`).
  - Non-string, empty, or C0/DEL/C1 control-character `acknowledge_degraded_at` values fail `situareness.json` validation (`ConfigEntryNotReady`). Unparseable/timezone-naive, over-length (`>100` characters), or future (`> reload time`) strings keep monitoring online, preserve `acknowledge_rejected: true` across healthy and mid-session cleared states, and surface reason-accurate `situareness_degraded` notices (including concurrently with `quarantine_ambiguous` and showing `expired_unsent_at <= acknowledge_degraded_at <= reload time` when latched `expired_unsent > 0`); premature (`< degraded_at` or `< expired_unsent_at`), `malformed_store`, or previously consumed timestamp strings do so when degraded or clearing latched `expired_unsent` (while re-latching an unrelated different degraded reason on Poll 1 does not flag `acknowledge_rejected: true`). A bounded `consumed_acknowledgements` history (`<= 100` valid ISO timestamps) merges across `entry.data` and store without downgrade so later retries never auto-apply them, and the triggering feed message or overflow condition must leave first (otherwise the next poll immediately re-latches with a new `degraded_at`).
  - Recover `malformed_store` only by repairing `.storage/situareness.<entry_id>`, deleting it and any `.corrupt.*` backups (or replacing its data with `{}`) and reloading (which enters `journal_lost`), or removing and re-adding the entry.
  - Resetting/replacing lost or corrupt stores or acknowledging `cancellation_evidence_overflow` early discards tombstones and can allow in-window alerts to re-evaluate or re-alert. Rely on independent official alert channels during any degraded state or after any store reset.

The integration loaded in an isolated Home Assistant 2026.11.1 test runtime; compatibility with a particular household installation and physical commissioning remain unverified.
