# Situareness for Home Assistant

This custom integration is uncommissioned. It independently polls NWS alerts once a minute and defaults to shadow mode. Actual installation and delivery require separate commissioning. Continue to use independent official alert channels. Provider and network delays, polling, controller outages and device behavior can delay or prevent an alert.

For a future authorized installation, copy `custom_components/situareness` from this archive to Home Assistant's `<config>/custom_components/situareness`, restart HA, then add **Situareness** under Settings > Devices & services. The optional `home-assistant/situareness.example.json` is a blank starting point for the private `<config>/situareness.json` file. Keep a local working copy under `ignored-resources/` and copy it privately to HA; reload the integration after changes. The example queries NY and DC and contains no configured footprints, rules, or recipients. Never share or commit private coordinates or device identifiers.

Configure private household longitude/latitude points under `locations`, never town centers. Optional location `name` supplies the display label and `speakers` contains HA media player entity IDs. Configure `approved_rules` as exact NWS event names mapped to reviewed starting phrases of official protective instructions. Unconfigured points, absent polygons, unmatched wording, and unapproved rules cannot produce an alarm. `phones` lists HA mobile-app service names without `notify.`; `tts_entity` identifies an HA TTS entity. Rules and targets require validation before relying on them.

Both `live_enabled` and `commissioning_passed` must be explicitly true before output services can be called. Both are false in the supplied example. Leave them false until policy replay, private footprint and source verification, coordinated phone and speaker testing, and failure/stop behavior are documented and separately authorized. These settings do not themselves prove commissioning. There is no horn, Ring, police/fire panic, acknowledgement or repeated-reminder adapter.

New qualifying alerts target all configured phones and speakers at affected locations only. Phone service data includes the official URL and is limited to 2,800 JSON bytes; long instructions are shortened with a prompt to open the source, and large location lists can be summarized. The full instruction remains in the private outbox. Each speaker names its own location, omits the URL, and limits spoken instructions to 300 characters with a prompt to open the source link if shortened. The budget leaves provider-envelope headroom but does not establish final APNs size or delivery.

The integration retries unsuccessful or uncertain per-target deliveries up to three times within fifteen minutes, after checking the alert is still current. Expiration-only updates do not trigger a new alarm. Before each bounded delivery group, the action window is rechecked against the current clock; a cancellation issued between source polls is not immediately known. A crash after HA accepts a service call but before acceptance is saved can result in a duplicate. Acceptance is not proof a person heard it.

The private persisted outbox (`Store(..., private=True)`) contains alert text and targets; it is deleted with the integration entry, while the separately managed configuration file remains. Temporarily absent or ineligible alerts suspend pending retries until equivalent current evidence qualifies again, for at most 15 minutes. A changed event or instruction invalidates earlier pending work. Alerts issued more than seven days ago (`MAX_ACCEPTANCE_AGE`) cannot qualify without a newer update, and persisted cancellation components remain protected for seven days from their latest component timestamp before aging out under the 1,000-record cap once absent. A cancellation blocks replay of explicitly referenced CAP messages; an uncertain pre-cancellation successor is suspended while the cancellation or uncertain record is current, valid continuing segments issued at or after a partial cancellation qualify normally for new actions (`state == "active"`) while pre-cancellation pending actions remain suspended, historic uncertainty clears once absent while any remaining suspended retry stays suspended until invalidated by a changed action or expired under the 15-minute outbox limit, and independently supported sibling branches can still qualify and follow their own updates. A later qualifying update explicitly referencing a valid earlier cancellation can establish a new action. These decisions do not establish a household all-clear.

Malformed, oversized, or conflicting duplicate non-`Cancel` features with an extractable `feature_id` quarantine scoped to `[feature_id, *valid_refs]` and report as partial while unrelated alerts and independently supported siblings continue; up to 5,000 unique quarantined identifiers (counting both feature IDs and reference IDs once per poll) are retained, and only an unextractable feature `id` or exceeding that 5,000-ID cap triggers `quarantine_ambiguous` (and raises `situareness_degraded`) for that poll. Transient fetch errors fail the poll without latching degraded state. If cancellation evidence exceeds retained capacity (`cancellation_evidence_overflow`, retaining active/current/`Cancel`/`cancelled` records ahead of ordinary history and extending `degraded_until` to cover evicted components' `dropped_horizon`), queued outbox targets exceed the 5,000-target limit (`outbox_overflow`, capping the persisted outbox at 5,000 targets), a malformed or oversized `Actual` `Cancel` lacks recoverable target references or exceeds the 5,000-identifier quarantine cap (`malformed_cancellation`), conflicting cancellation evidence lacks recoverable targets or exceeds 5,000 IDs (`conflicting_cancellation`), or a previously initialized config entry's store goes missing (`journal_lost`), the sensor reports `evidence_degraded: true`, `output_blocked: true`, `degraded_reason`, `degraded_at`, `degraded_until`, and an unknown active count, raises the `situareness_degraded` HA persistent notification, and continues read-only collection (without marking withheld plans as delivered or burning outbox retry age on active pre-degradation targets) while blocking output until the triggering condition clears and either `degraded_until` expires or an administrator sets a one-shot `acknowledge_degraded_at` in `situareness.json` and reloads the integration (`acknowledge_degraded_at` is evaluated only at load/reload against `degraded_at <= acknowledge_degraded_at <= load_now`, so future-dated timestamps do not auto-clear on later polls). While an active feed trigger (`malformed_cancellation`, `conflicting_cancellation`) or >1,000-record capacity overflow (`cancellation_evidence_overflow`) remains present, each poll refreshes `degraded_at` and `degraded_until` (never shrinking an earlier longer `degraded_until`), preventing premature recovery; `outbox_overflow` caps the persisted outbox at 5,000 targets when triggered and recovers after `degraded_until` expires or load-time `acknowledge_degraded_at` clears it (re-triggering only if post-recovery plans again push the outbox above 5,000). A deep-validated malformed or unparseable/corrupt JSON journal (`malformed_store`, including HA's `.corrupt.*` backup files and marking `journal_initialized` on any non-empty or malformed store load) persists a stable `degraded_at` (`degraded_until: null`), withholds output, raises `situareness_degraded`, and skips collection; `acknowledge_degraded_at` does not clear `malformed_store`, which requires repairing the persisted store JSON on disk and reloading, deleting the store file (and any `.corrupt.*` backup files) on disk (or replacing its data with empty JSON `{}`) and reloading (transitioning to `journal_lost`), or removing and re-adding the integration entry (which deletes the corrupt store and backups and dismisses `situareness_degraded`). Resetting or replacing a corrupt or lost store (`malformed_store` / `journal_lost`), or acknowledging `cancellation_evidence_overflow` early, discards or truncates historical tombstones and may allow previously seen or cancelled alerts still in their acceptance window to re-evaluate or re-alert. Continue to rely on independent official sources while degraded. The sensor distinguishes all pending, currently deliverable, and suspended items, and excludes `events`, `last_success`, `degraded_at`, and `degraded_until` from recorder history. HA sensor attributes contain redacted status and event summaries, not coordinates or device IDs.

The integration loaded in an isolated Home Assistant 2026.11.1 test runtime; compatibility with a particular household installation and physical commissioning remain unverified.
