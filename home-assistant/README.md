# Situareness for Home Assistant

This custom integration is uncommissioned. It independently polls NWS alerts once a minute and defaults to shadow mode. Actual installation and delivery require separate commissioning. Continue to use independent official alert channels. Provider and network delays, polling, controller outages and device behavior can delay or prevent an alert.

For a future authorized installation, copy `custom_components/situareness` from this archive to Home Assistant's `<config>/custom_components/situareness`, restart HA, then add **Situareness** under Settings > Devices & services. The optional `home-assistant/situareness.example.json` is a blank starting point for the private `<config>/situareness.json` file. Keep a local working copy under `ignored-resources/` and copy it privately to HA; reload the integration after changes. The example queries NY and DC and contains no configured footprints, rules, or recipients. Never share or commit private coordinates or device identifiers.

Configure private household longitude/latitude points under `locations`, never town centers. Optional location `name` supplies the display label and `speakers` contains HA media player entity IDs. Configure `approved_rules` as exact NWS event names mapped to reviewed starting phrases of official protective instructions. Unconfigured points, absent polygons, unmatched wording, and unapproved rules cannot produce an alarm. `phones` lists HA mobile-app service names without `notify.`; `tts_entity` identifies an HA TTS entity. Rules and targets require validation before relying on them.

Both `live_enabled` and `commissioning_passed` must be explicitly true before output services can be called. Both are false in the supplied example. Leave them false until policy replay, private footprint and source verification, coordinated phone and speaker testing, and failure/stop behavior are documented and separately authorized. These settings do not themselves prove commissioning. There is no horn, Ring, police/fire panic, acknowledgement or repeated-reminder adapter.

New qualifying alerts target all configured phones and speakers at affected locations only. Phone service data includes the official URL and is limited to 2,800 JSON bytes; long instructions are shortened with a prompt to open the source, and large location lists can be summarized. The full instruction remains in the private outbox. Each speaker names its own location, omits the URL, and limits spoken instructions to 300 characters with a prompt to open the source link if shortened. The budget leaves provider-envelope headroom but does not establish final APNs size or delivery.

The integration prioritizes new first-attempt targets ahead of retries and retries unsuccessful or uncertain per-target deliveries up to three times within fifteen minutes (`MAX_AGE`), after checking the alert is still current (`100` target attempts/poll, `30`-second deadline, persisting once before a 10-target group when expired items are pruned and once after each completed 10-target group; overflow retries above ~1,500 targets can expire unsent). During non-`outbox_overflow` degraded modes (`7`-day+ holds), `created` ages freeze via `last_degraded_poll`, so pre-degradation retries can deliver days later once degradation clears if the alert remains active and qualifying. Expiration-only updates do not trigger a new alarm. Before each bounded delivery group, the action window is rechecked against the current clock; a cancellation issued between source polls is not immediately known. A crash after HA accepts a service call but before persistence can duplicate that group. Acceptance is not proof a person heard it.

## Health, storage, and degraded recovery

- **Sensor state and attributes:** `sensor.situareness_monitoring` exposes `active_count`, `event_count`, `source_feature_count`, `last_success`, `shadow`, `planned_count`, `withheld_count`, `pending_count`, `deliverable_count`, `suspended_count`, `delivery_attempts`, `delivery_failures`, `partial`, `quarantined_count`, `quarantine_ambiguous`, `cancellation_uncertain`, `evidence_degraded`, `output_blocked`, `degraded_reason`, `degraded_at`, `degraded_until`, `acknowledge_rejected`, and up to 25 redacted `events` (excluding `events`, `last_success`, `degraded_at`, and `degraded_until` from recorder history).
- **Private storage and bounds:** State uses Home Assistant's `Store(..., private=True)` (`0600` when supported); graph reconciliation and store schema checks run via `async_add_executor_job`. Removing the entry dismisses notifications before deleting the journal and any `.corrupt.*` backups while keeping `situareness.json`. Caps are `1,000` records (retention bounded at `min(ts, updated + 5m) + 7d` `MAX_ACCEPTANCE_AGE` and clamped at `MAX_TIMESTAMP`), `100` references/alert, `100` fingerprints/record, `5,000` unique quarantined IDs/poll, and `5,000` outbox targets. Scoped quarantine isolates malformed/oversized features with valid IDs; continuing segments at or after a partial cancellation (`sent >= cancelled_at`) can qualify new actions while pre-cancellation retries stay suspended.
- **Evidence and storage degraded modes (`evidence_degraded: true`, `output_blocked: true`, `active_count: unknown`):** Triggered by `cancellation_evidence_overflow` (`>1,000` protected records; `degraded_until` at least `7` days and covers evicted protected/cancellation component horizons up to `MAX_TIMESTAMP`), `malformed_cancellation` / `conflicting_cancellation` (an `Actual Cancel` lacks recoverable target references or exceeds the `5,000`-ID quarantine cap, or conflicting cancellation evidence lacks recoverable scope; `7` days), `journal_lost` (missing store on an initialized entry; persisted pre-fetch with `7` days from detection), or `malformed_store` (corrupt JSON, `.corrupt.*` backups, unsupported major version, outbox `>5,000`, or schema error; skips collection and writes with `degraded_until: null`; transient `OSError` retries via `ConfigEntryNotReady`). Poll-wide `quarantine_ambiguous` (including `>5,000` quarantined non-Cancel IDs) suspends all lineages for that poll and raises `situareness_degraded`.
- **Capacity mode (`outbox_overflow`, `evidence_degraded: false`, `output_blocked: false`, `partial: true`):** When queued plus new targets exceed `5,000`, drains `100` deliverable targets first if available, queues whole plans atomically up to `5,000`, strips/withholds overflowing plans (`withheld_count`), continues bounded `100`-target/poll queued delivery (first-attempt items ahead of retries) under a `15`-minute `degraded_until` without freezing item age, and auto-clears once capacity fits.
- **Operator recovery (`acknowledge_degraded_at` and `.storage`):**
  - While the same non-`malformed_store` reason persists, `degraded_at` stays at initial latch time while `degraded_until` extends.
  - Non-`malformed_store` modes clear when `degraded_until` expires on a clean poll, or on reload after setting a non-future timezone-aware ISO-8601 `acknowledge_degraded_at` in `situareness.json` with `degraded_at <= acknowledge_degraded_at <= reload time`.
  - Non-string, empty, or control-character `acknowledge_degraded_at` values fail `situareness.json` validation (`ConfigEntryNotReady`). Unparseable/timezone-naive, over-length (`>100` characters), or future (`> reload time`) strings keep monitoring online, set `acknowledge_rejected: true`, and surface `situareness_degraded` even while healthy; premature (`< degraded_at`), `malformed_store`, or reused timestamp strings do so while degraded. Valid ISO timestamps are consumed in entry/store state so later retries never auto-apply them, and the triggering feed message or overflow condition must leave first (otherwise the next poll immediately re-latches with a new `degraded_at`).
  - Recover `malformed_store` only by repairing `.storage/situareness.<entry_id>`, deleting it and any `.corrupt.*` backups (or replacing its data with `{}`) and reloading (which enters `journal_lost`), or removing and re-adding the entry.
  - Resetting/replacing lost or corrupt stores or acknowledging `cancellation_evidence_overflow` early discards tombstones and can allow in-window alerts to re-evaluate or re-alert. Rely on independent official alert channels during any degraded state or after any store reset.

The integration loaded in an isolated Home Assistant 2026.11.1 test runtime; compatibility with a particular household installation and physical commissioning remain unverified.
