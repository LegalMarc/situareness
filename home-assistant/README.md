# Situareness for Home Assistant

This custom integration is uncommissioned. It independently polls NWS alerts once a minute and defaults to shadow mode. Actual installation and delivery require separate commissioning. Continue to use independent official alert channels. Provider and network delays, polling, controller outages and device behavior can delay or prevent an alert.

For a future authorized installation, copy `custom_components/situareness` from this archive to Home Assistant's `<config>/custom_components/situareness`, restart HA, then add **Situareness** under Settings > Devices & services. The optional `home-assistant/situareness.example.json` is a blank starting point for the private `<config>/situareness.json` file. Keep a local working copy under `ignored-resources/` and copy it privately to HA; reload the integration after changes. The example queries NY and DC and contains no configured footprints, rules, or recipients. Never share or commit private coordinates or device identifiers.

Configure private household longitude/latitude points under `locations`, never town centers. Optional location `name` supplies the display label and `speakers` contains HA media player entity IDs. Configure `approved_rules` as exact NWS event names mapped to reviewed starting phrases of official protective instructions. Unconfigured points, absent polygons, unmatched wording, and unapproved rules cannot produce an alarm. `phones` lists HA mobile-app service names without `notify.`; `tts_entity` identifies an HA TTS entity. Rules and targets require validation before relying on them.

Both `live_enabled` and `commissioning_passed` must be explicitly true before output services can be called. Both are false in the supplied example. Leave them false until policy replay, private footprint and source verification, coordinated phone and speaker testing, and failure/stop behavior are documented and separately authorized. These settings do not themselves prove commissioning. There is no horn, Ring, police/fire panic, recipient alert acknowledgement, or repeated-reminder adapter.

New qualifying alerts target all configured phones and speakers at affected locations only. Phone service data includes the official URL and is limited to 2,800 JSON bytes; long instructions are shortened with a prompt to open the source, and large location lists can be summarized. The full instruction remains in the private outbox. Each speaker names its own location, omits the URL, and limits spoken instructions to 300 characters with a prompt to open the source link if shortened. The budget leaves provider-envelope headroom but does not establish final APNs size or delivery.

- **Collection and delivery bounds:** The integration queries NWS active GeoJSON alerts across configured states under `50`s total fetch timeout, `25`s per-page timeout, `50` pages, and `8 MiB`/page response limits, deduplicating identical feature copies without failing large multi-state bursts. It prioritizes new first-attempt targets ahead of retries and makes up to three total delivery attempts per target (`MAX_ATTEMPTS = 3`: one initial call plus up to two retries for failed or uncertain calls, not repeated reminders) within fifteen minutes (`MAX_AGE`), after rechecking the current clock before each 10-target delivery group (`100` attempts/poll, `30`-second deadline).
- **Pre-drain, serialization cost, and unsent expirations:** When a near-capacity poll above `5,000` projected targets pre-drains existing deliverable items before queueing new plans, newly queued first-attempt items deliver using any remaining `100 - pre_drain_attempts` budget within the 30-second deadline (or wait until the next poll if exhausted). Outbox items store up to 10KB instructions, so a 5,000-target outbox can serialize tens of MB across up to `11` saves/poll normally, or up to `22` saves/poll when pre-drain runs a second delivery pass. Unexhausted queued targets for a currently active (or suspended-from-active) alert invalidated by `15`-minute expiry (`age`, e.g., above ~1,500 attempts/15m or slow timeouts), backward clock rollback (`created > now`), or removed recipients in `situareness.json` are surfaced with their cause via persisted `expired_unsent`, `expired_unsent_reasons`, `partial: true`, `outcomes` (`expired: true`, `reason`), and `situareness_degraded` latched for seven days or until acknowledged on reload.
- **Expiration updates, degradation freezes, and caveats:** During non-`outbox_overflow` degraded modes (`7`-day+ holds), `created` ages freeze via `last_degraded_poll` so surviving pre-degradation retries can deliver once recovery completes. Expiration-only updates do not trigger a new alarm, `ended_or_absent` transitions do not establish official cancellation (learned only on a later source poll), and a crash after HA accepts a service call before persistence can duplicate that group. Acceptance is not proof a person heard it.

## Health, storage, and degraded recovery

- **Sensor state and attributes:** `sensor.situareness_monitoring` exposes `active_count`, `event_count`, `source_feature_count`, `last_success`, `shadow`, `planned_count`, `withheld_count`, `pending_count`, `deliverable_count`, `suspended_count`, `delivery_attempts`, `delivery_failures`, `expired_unsent`, `partial`, `quarantined_count`, `quarantine_ambiguous`, `cancellation_uncertain`, `evidence_degraded`, `output_blocked`, `degraded_reason`, `degraded_at`, `degraded_until`, `acknowledge_rejected`, and up to 25 redacted `events` (excluding `events`, `last_success`, `degraded_at`, and `degraded_until` from recorder history).
- **Private storage and offload:** State uses Home Assistant's `Store(..., private=True)` (`0600` when supported); async HTTP pagination runs in `collector.fetch()` while per-page JSON decoding and feature normalization/quarantine offload via `asyncio.to_thread`, post-fetch coordinator CPU work (`_valid_persisted_store`, candidate resolution, `reconcile`, outbox migration/planning, `_strip_withheld_actions`, deliverable-count checks, and deliverable batch prefiltering/sorting) runs via `async_add_executor_job`, and per-group `<=10`-item `qualify`/`pending_valid` delivery rechecks run on the event loop. Every persisted record `state` (and non-`null` `prior_state`) is validated against `{active, ineligible, ended_or_absent, quarantined, unconfirmed, superseded, cancelled}` plus optional `expired_unsent_reasons` / outcome `"reason"` values. Removing the entry dismisses notifications before deleting the journal and `.corrupt.*` backups while keeping `situareness.json`; the first successful post-`malformed_store` save automatically deletes stale `.corrupt.*` backups (or logs a warning and retries without misclassifying later `journal_lost` if unlink fails). Unloading stops the coordinator before unloading platforms, `_stopping` prevents post-fetch saves/delivery once stopped, and abandoned or failed initial setups (`SETUP_RETRY` transitioning to `not_loaded`, `setup_error`, or `migration_error`) dismiss `situareness_degraded`.
- **Bounds and quarantine scoping:** Caps are `1,000` records (`min(ts, updated + 5m) + 7d` `MAX_ACCEPTANCE_AGE` clamped at `MAX_TIMESTAMP`), `100` references/alert, `100` fingerprints/record, `5,000` unique quarantined IDs/poll, and `5,000` outbox targets. `_bound_records()` protects cancellation components, `active`/`cancelled`/`prior_state == "active"`/`<=15m`-bridge-action lineages, and scoped non-`quarantine_all` `quarantined` lineages even when absent, while excluding poll-wide `quarantine_all` / `unconfirmed` suspensions without active prior state or `<=15m` actions, ordinary `ineligible` items, and non-actionable hidden `superseded` placeholders outside actionable/cancellation groups (so >1,000-feature ambiguous bursts truncate cleanly without false overflow, with safe snapshot fallback for evicted `ineligible` items). Scoped quarantine isolates malformed/oversized features with valid IDs (`[feature_id, *absent_unreferenced_valid_refs]` for non-`Cancel` features, while only `Actual` `Update`/`Cancel` messages can supersede referenced alerts); continuing segments at or after a partial cancellation (`sent >= cancelled_at`) can qualify new actions while pre-cancellation retries stay suspended.
- **Evidence and storage degraded modes (`evidence_degraded: true`, `output_blocked: true`, `active_count: unknown`):** Triggered by `cancellation_evidence_overflow` (`>1,000` protected records; `degraded_until >= 7d` covering evicted component horizons up to `MAX_TIMESTAMP`), `malformed_cancellation` / `conflicting_cancellation` (unrecoverable `Actual Cancel` targets, >5,000 quarantine cap, or conflicting cancellation scope), `journal_lost` (missing store on an initialized entry persisted pre-fetch clamped to `MAX_TIMESTAMP`), or `malformed_store` (corrupt JSON, `.corrupt.*` backups when not already recovered, unsupported major version, outbox `>5,000`, or schema error; skips collection/writes with `degraded_until: null`; `OSError` retries via `ConfigEntryNotReady`). Higher-precedence active evidence reasons (`conflicting_cancellation > malformed_cancellation > journal_lost > cancellation_evidence_overflow`) keep `degraded_reason` precedence over lower-precedence concurrent triggers while extending `degraded_until` to `max(..., overflow_horizon)`. Poll-wide `quarantine_ambiguous` suspends all lineages for that poll without counting ordinary `quarantine_all` suspensions toward the 1,000-record overflow cap and raises `situareness_degraded`.
- **Capacity mode (`outbox_overflow`, `evidence_degraded: false`, `output_blocked: false`, `partial: true`):** When queued plus newly planned targets exceed `5,000`, drains `100` deliverable targets first if available, queues whole plans with non-empty `new_items` atomically up to `5,000` (already-queued empty-`new_items` plans are never withheld or stripped), strips/withholds only overflowing plans (`withheld_count`), continues any remaining per-poll budget within `100` attempts/`30s`, continues bounded queued delivery under a `15`-minute `degraded_until` without freezing item age, and auto-clears once capacity fits.
- **Operator recovery (`acknowledge_degraded_at` and `.storage`):**
  - While any non-`outbox_overflow` evidence/storage degradation persists (or the same `outbox_overflow` reason persists), `degraded_at` stays at initial latch time while `degraded_until` extends.
  - Non-`malformed_store` modes clear when `degraded_until` expires on a clean poll, or on reload after setting a non-future timezone-aware ISO-8601 `acknowledge_degraded_at` in `situareness.json` with `degraded_at <= acknowledge_degraded_at <= reload time` (latched `expired_unsent` clears after seven days, or alongside `self.degraded` / while healthy on reload only when `expired_unsent_at` is present and `expired_unsent_at <= acknowledge_degraded_at <= reload time`; when `expired_unsent_at` is missing or later, clearing `self.degraded` leaves `expired_unsent` latched).
  - Non-string, empty, or C0/DEL/C1 control-character `acknowledge_degraded_at` values fail `situareness.json` validation (`ConfigEntryNotReady`).
  - At load time, unparseable/timezone-naive, over-length (`>100` characters), or future (`> reload time`) strings keep monitoring online, preserve `acknowledge_rejected: true` across healthy and mid-session cleared states, and surface reason-accurate `situareness_degraded` notices (including concurrently with `quarantine_ambiguous`); premature (`< degraded_at` or `< expired_unsent_at`), missing-anchor `expired_unsent`, `malformed_store`, or already-consumed values also reject when degraded or clearing latched `expired_unsent`, while re-latching an unrelated different reason on Poll 1 does not flag `acknowledge_rejected: true`. Bounded `<=100`-entry `consumed_acknowledgements` history merges across `entry.data` and store without downgrade, and triggering feed/overflow conditions must clear before permanent reload recovery.
  - Recover `malformed_store` only by repairing `.storage/situareness.<entry_id>`, deleting it and any `.corrupt.*` backups (or replacing its data with `{}`) and reloading (which enters `journal_lost`), or removing and re-adding the entry.
  - Resetting/replacing lost or corrupt stores or acknowledging `cancellation_evidence_overflow` early discards tombstones and can allow in-window alerts to re-evaluate or re-alert. Rely on independent official alert channels during any degraded state or after any store reset.

The integration loaded in an isolated Home Assistant 2026.11.1 test runtime; compatibility with a particular household installation and physical commissioning remain unverified.
